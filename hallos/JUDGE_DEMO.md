# HallOS — 60-Second Hackathon Demo

Prepare a **fictional demo hall**, two approved demonstration accounts (resident and RA), a stable HTTPS app link, and a QR sign generated from the deployed app. Never use genuine private resident records for the demonstration.

| Time | Demo action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | “GroupMe messages get buried. Scan this QR at the shared bathroom.” Scan with resident phone. | Real-world entry point, no app-navigation hunt |
| 10–22 s | Resident sees a fictional bathroom issue, taps **Me too**, and the confirmation count rises. | Reduces repetitive messages and duplicate issue tickets |
| 22–35 s | Switch to approved RA view. Staff desk shows the same issue; Hall Radar shows its location. | Two connected user roles, one real data flow |
| 35–48 s | Open Visitation. Staff posts a confirmed closing time to HallCast as a manual hall-wide alert. | Relevant, staff-verified updates instead of chat noise |
| 48–60 s | Return to resident phone. Open Inbox or wait for app-open alert banner. | Personalized hall notifications with a visible result |

**Pitch:** “GroupMe tells you what’s happening. HallOS helps your entire residence hall do something about it.”

## Be precise for judges

- Demo messages, halls, residents and reports are fictional.
- HallOS is NOT Morgan's official maintenance or emergency communication system.
- Only in-app alert delivery is implemented. The app does not have reliable background phone push or SMS.
- QR signs should only be placed in a hall with staff authorization.
- Base44 account security, permissions and live integrations must be validated before a production pilot.
