# Morgan State references behind HallOS V10
Researched November 5, 2026. Always recheck official sources before displaying emergency numbers in production.

1. **University-managed housing list**: https://www.morgan.edu/residence-life-and-housing/our-housing-options
   - On campus: Baldwin Hall, Blount Towers, Cummings House, Legacy Hall, Rawlings Hall, Thurgood Marshall Hall.
   - University-managed / leased off campus: Altus Apartments, The Enolia, HH Midtown (The Varsity), Marble Hall Gardens, One Calvert Plaza, Lord Baltimore Hotel as the temporary Harper-Tubman placement.
   - The Harper-Tubman building itself is undergoing renovation for Fall 2026 and is not labeled as an operating hall in V10.

2. **Housing agreement (on/off campus categories)**: https://www.morgan.edu/residence-life-and-housing/housing-and-food-service-agreement

3. **Residential facilities, work-order portals, RA on-call**: https://www.morgan.edu/orlhfacilities
   - Traditional on-campus halls → MorganReady through Facilities Management.
   - Legacy Hall → Legacy Hall Resident Portal.
   - Thurgood Marshall Hall → Thurgood Marshall Resident Portal.
   - Altus → Altus Resident Portal.
   - The Enolia → its property portal (Homebody).
   - HH Midtown → Morgan-listed maintenance request URL.
   - Marble Hall and Lord Baltimore → official facilities guidance page, since the available redirect needs confirmation.
   - One Calvert Plaza → Morgan-linked resident Airtable request form.
   - After-hours RA numbers included only where the university has published them.
   - For urgent issues including active leaks and power outages, contact residence director / front desk immediately.

4. **Housing and RA staff roles**: https://www.morgan.edu/residence-life-and-housing/meet-the-staff
   - Morgan confirms the RA and Resident Director structure. HallOS does **not** claim to know the specific RA assigned to each floor.

5. **BEARtransit off-campus residential schedules**: https://www.morgan.edu/business-and-auxiliary-services/beartransit/off-campus-residential-schedules
   - A link to the current official schedules; HallOS does not infer shuttle arrival times or publish unsupported live ETAs.

6. **Harper-Tubman housing placement**: https://www.morgan.edu/residence-life-and-housing/our-housing-options/harper-tubman

7. **Separate Morgan View application scope**: https://www.morgan.edu/residence-life-and-housing/apply-for-housing/transfer-student-housing
   - Morgan View requires separate application. Not automatically claimed as a university-managed HallOS RA hall.

8. **Rawlings hall contact details**: https://www.morgan.edu/residence-life-and-housing/our-housing-options/rawlings-hall

Every local HallOS report, profile and shared space remains explicitly labeled fictional. There is no authorized API connection to MorganReady or resident property systems.
